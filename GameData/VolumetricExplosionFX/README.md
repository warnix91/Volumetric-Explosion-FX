# Volumetric Explosion FX v1.1

Visual explosion effects for KSP 1.12.5, by Warnix. Gameplay, physics and sound are unchanged.

## Installation

Close KSP, then copy `VolumetricExplosionFX` into `GameData`.
When updating, keep `PluginData/settings.cfg` to retain your settings.
To uninstall, remove `GameData/VolumetricExplosionFX`.

## Settings

Use the flame toolbar button in flight or at the Space Center.
Settings are saved to `PluginData/settings.cfg`.
The interface follows the language selected in KSP.

Quality defaults to Ultra. Existing saved settings take precedence.
Pick the Performance quick setup or a lower quality if needed.
Photo mode (Alt+F7) slows down or freezes the explosions while the game runs.
The 1.0 look stays available: "Compare with 1.0" in the look editor.
